Essay · Trust Systems

The Trust Index — why we buy from friends of friends

You wouldn't buy something from a complete stranger — the biggest problem is trust. But you wouldn't hesitate to buy from your friend's friend, or someone from your office, even if you don't know them personally. I'd even trust someone from a friend's company.

That observation led to an idea: a trusted network of individuals who transact among each other. People join only by invitation, and everyone carries a rating. Your rating grows as you transact on the network — and shrinks when you, or someone you invited, acts fraudulently.

Handling a bad actor means decreasing the rating of the actor and the people who invited them, with the penalty decaying as you move up the invite tree. Trust becomes transitive, measurable, and — most importantly — expensive to lose.

Related idea: Trusted Buy & Sell Network · working name Apex Guild
